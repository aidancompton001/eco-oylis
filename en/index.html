Frequently asked questions

How is the collection of used oils carried out?

The company provides specialized containers from 20 to 1000 liters and organizes regular collection on an individual schedule tailored to each partner's needs.

What containers does the company provide?

The company provides reusable specialized containers with a capacity from 20 to 1000 liters, suitable for various types of establishments and food producers.

What documentation is provided for environmental control?

The company provides full documentation — contracts, transport invoices, and volume reports required for environmental control and regulatory compliance.

What is a Trade Representation?

The Trade Representation is a non-legal entity without the right to conduct commercial activity in the Republic of Bulgaria. It represents the interests of LLC EKO-OYLIS, Ukraine, and facilitates the establishment of partnership relations.
