4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds under the GDPR:

  • Article 6(1)(a) — Consent: When you voluntarily submit data via our contact form or accept analytics/marketing cookies.
  • Article 6(1)(b) — Contractual necessity: When processing is necessary to discuss or enter into a partnership or service agreement.
  • Article 6(1)(f) — Legitimate interest: For website security, fraud prevention, and improving our services.
  • Article 6(1)(c) — Legal obligation: When required by Bulgarian or EU law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above:

  • Contact form data: Up to 2 years from the date of your last enquiry, unless a business relationship is established.
  • Analytics data: In accordance with Google Analytics data retention settings (default: 14 months).
  • Legal/contractual obligations: As required by applicable law (typically 5 years under Bulgarian commercial law).

6. Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google Analytics uses cookies to help us analyse how visitors use the site. The information generated by the cookie about your use of the website is generally transmitted to a Google server in the EU/EEA and stored there.

We have enabled IP anonymisation, so your IP address is shortened by Google within the EU/EEA before transmission. Google will use this information to evaluate your use of the website, compile reports on website activity, and provide other services relating to website and internet usage.

You can prevent the collection of data by Google Analytics by adjusting your cookie preferences or by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Your Rights Under the GDPR

As a data subject, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — You may request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction (Art. 18) — You may request restriction of processing.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — You may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — You may object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at office@eko-oylis.com.ua.
